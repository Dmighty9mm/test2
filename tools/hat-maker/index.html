PlebMc Hat Maker

Build a hat out of blocks on your head, then download a Blockbench model PlebioAPI can sell. One block here is one pixel of a Minecraft head.

1Tool

Click a face to use the tool. Drag empty space or right-drag to turn the view, scroll to zoom. 1-4 tools, M mirror, Ctrl+Z undo.

2Color

Pick any color. It joins the palette.

3Start from

4Download


        
Put the .bbmodel in plugins/PlebioAPI/hats/, add the line to items: in config.yml and run /pleb reload. You can also open the model in Blockbench to keep editing.
