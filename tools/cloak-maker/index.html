PLEBMC CLOAK MAKER

Turn a GIF, a few frames, or one picture into an animated cloak for the PlebMc shop. You get one PNG and the line to paste into the PlebioAPI config.

1 Art

Drop a GIF, or one or more images
Several images become frames in name order (frame01.png, frame02.png ...). One still image can be animated with the effects in step 2.

Or start from a generated effect, no art needed:

Colors drive the effects and the shine and sparkle overlays.

2 Motion

GIFs keep their own frames and timing (up to 64 frames). The frames slider is for effects and overlays.
